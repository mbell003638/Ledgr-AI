## App name

Ledgr

## Short description

Offline accounting, inventory and reports for small businesses.

## Full description

Run your business books from one clear, local-first accounting app.

Ledgr helps shops, service businesses, freelancers and partnerships record everyday transactions and understand their financial position without depending on a developer-operated cloud service.

Key features:

- Record sales, purchases, invoices, receipts, payments and expenses
- Track customers, suppliers, debtors and creditors
- Maintain cash, inventory, assets, liabilities and partner capital
- View Cash Book, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet and Trial Balance reports
- Close accounting periods using physical inventory counts
- Calculate manager commission and carry balances forward
- Manage multiple business books
- Export and restore complete JSON backups
- Create and share PDFs and summaries
- Use optional voice, OCR and AI assistance with your selected provider

Accounting records are stored on your device. Core bookkeeping works offline. AI tools are optional and send selected content directly to the provider you configure only when invoked.

Ledgr is a bookkeeping and record-management tool. It does not provide banking, lending, investment, insurance, cryptocurrency, money-transfer or payment-processing services.
