Ledgr-AI

Master Product & Engineering Prompt

Mission

Build Ledgr-AI as a polished, offline-first, on-device accounting app for global small businesses, shops, service businesses, freelancers, and partnerships. It must be easy for non-technical users through AI and voice assistance while remaining accountant-correct, auditable, and suitable for Play Store release.

Standalone: bookkeeping data stays on the user's device. No hosted backend is required for core bookkeeping.

Personas & onboarding

Retail / wholesale

Inventory, purchases, stock counts, COGS, suppliers.

Service businesses

Salon, handyman, repair, professional service workflows.

IT / freelancer

Clients, projects, time/service records, invoices.

Custom

User-selected modules and custom terminology.

Onboarding supports multiple personas. Personas can be added or removed later without destroying data. Each persona controls visible modules, terminology, fields, defaults, reports, and suggested account structure.

Multiple books & accounting styles

Users can maintain independent books/accounts on one device and toggle the active book in Settings. Each book has isolated transactions, settings, opening balances, members, personas, reports, and period closes.

Offer Standard accrual, Standard cash, Retail periodic-inventory partnership, Simple cashbook, and Custom/hybrid styles. The selected style must use the same authoritative ledger and reporting engine.

Retail partnership mode

  • Two investors can have different opening capital but a configured 50/50 profit share.
  • Shopkeeper salary/expense plus commission on positive gross profit; commission can vary by period.
  • Irregular physical inventory counts.
  • COGS = opening inventory + purchases − closing inventory.
  • Net Profit = Gross Profit − commission − operating expenses. Drawings are not expenses.
  • Closing capital = opening capital + allocated net profit − drawings.
  • Close Books creates an immutable period snapshot and carries closing balances forward.

Double-entry rules

EventRequired posting
Credit saleInvoice linked to stable party ID; Debit Accounts Receivable; Credit Sales Revenue; no cash until receipt.
Cash saleDebit selected payment account; Credit Sales Revenue.
Customer receiptDebit cash/bank/card account; Credit Accounts Receivable for validated allocations. Accrual revenue does not repeat.
AdvanceDebit payment account; Credit Customer Advances. Applying it reduces advance liability and receivable.
PurchaseCash purchase reduces payment account; credit purchase increases Accounts Payable.
ExpenseExpense reduces profit; paid expense reduces selected account; unpaid expense creates payable.
DrawingReduces equity only, never P&L.

Use stable bookId, partyId, periodId, and source-document IDs. Never rely on mutable names for accounting links.

Reports & Custom Reports

Dashboard, Sales, Receipts, Purchases, Cash/Bank Book, Day Book, Debtor/Creditor statements, P&L, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, tax, inventory/COGS, period close, and Custom Reports must derive from one posting engine and reconcile.

Replace the fixed Staff report with a Custom Report Builder. The user selects period, sections, fields, grouping, sorting, branding, and print orientation. Export PDF, HTML, and text, then share through Android share sheet/WhatsApp. AI can explain any report.

AI & voice-first UX

Commands such as “Record a cash sale of 500”, “Create a credit invoice”, “Show unpaid customers”, “What is net profit this period?”, and “Close books” must use the same validated API as the UI. Financial writes require confirmation. AI never writes raw collections directly.

Professional UI

  • Consistent cards, tiles, typography, spacing, icons, empty/loading/error states.
  • Persona-driven navigation that hides irrelevant modules.
  • Consistent detail views with Edit, Delete, Duplicate, Share, Print, and More actions.
  • Light/dark theme, accessible labels, strong contrast, large touch targets, undo/confirmation.

Acceptance tests

  • Credit sale → Party → Invoice → Debtor → Receivable.
  • Cash sale → selected payment account → Sales.
  • Partial receipt, Mark Paid, overpayment, advance application.
  • Payment edit/delete reversal and invoice edit/party rename.
  • Cash vs bank/card, cash vs credit purchase, supplier payment, expense, notes.
  • COGS, drawings, capital close, multiple books/personas.
  • All reports reconcile; debit total equals credit total; Assets = Liabilities + Equity.
  • AI and voice use the same validated write API.

Delivery discipline

Plan first. Implement in verified batches. Run npx tsc --noEmit and full npx jest after every batch. Use independent review for accounting changes. Never push or build until the branch is clean, the remote SHA is verified, and all acceptance tests pass.
